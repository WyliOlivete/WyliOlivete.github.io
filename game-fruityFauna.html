How it plays

Tap fruit. Make friends. Strike gold.

Win them over, unlock their story

Every animal has its own voice. A toucan who won't stop talking about his bill. A binturong who smells like buttered popcorn (really). Each win builds Affection, and every Affection level unlocks more of their story, inspired by how they really live and told in their own words.

Real animals, real homes

Meet them where they live, across 13 researched habitats: rainforest canopy and understory, mangrove coasts, swamp forest, savanna, desert scrub, boreal forest, rocky cliffs and more.

Rare in the wild, rare in the game

Animals visit as often as their real conservation status allows. Least Concern animals drop by all the time. Critically Endangered ones, like the Bornean Orangutan, are a rare sight. Fill your Bestiary from Common all the way to Legendary.

Grow your bond, grow your power

Match enough fruit to plant special flowers that spread across the board. The more an animal likes you, the stronger your flowers grow.

Finish their Cravings, strike gold

Complete an animal's Cravings and every win ends in a Gold Rush, where the whole board turns to coins.

Meet the cast

Each animal's Bestiary tier follows its real IUCN Red List status, and endangered animals turn up less often on your board.

Questions

Is Fruity Fauna free?

Yes. Fruity Fauna is free to download and play. It has optional in-app purchases and ads.

Do I need an internet connection?

No for the core puzzles: they play offline. You need a connection to sign in, back up your progress to the cloud, add friends and see the leaderboard.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play right away as a guest. Create an account (email sign-in) only if you want cloud backup and friends.

What changed in the big update?

Gold is now called Coins and Diamonds are now Gems. Energy is gone, so you can play as many puzzles as you like. Habitat decorating has been retired so the game can focus on the animals.

I bought something before the update. What happens to it?

Your past purchases are kept. If anything you bought no longer has a use, email service.wyliolivete@gmail.com and I'll make it right with Coins and Gems.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Social menu and tap Delete account. Can't open the game? See Delete your Fruity Fauna account.
