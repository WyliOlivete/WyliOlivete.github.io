Fruity Fauna by Wyli

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Fruity Fauna (on the App Store and Google Play) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. Fruity Fauna is made and published by Wyli Charles Olivete (“Wyli”, “I”, “me”), an independent developer based in the Philippines.

Questions or requests: service.wyliolivete@gmail.com.

Summary

  • You can play without an account. Guest progress stays on your device.
  • If you create an account, your email and game progress are stored with Google Firebase so you can back up your game and play with friends.
  • Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a reward. Ad services use your device's advertising ID.
  • The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the game is played, such as which boards are hard and which screens are used. This isn't linked to your email or account.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. I never see your payment details.
  • I don't sell your personal information.

1. Information collected

a. If you play as a guest

Your progress (animals met, Affection, Bestiary, currencies and settings) is saved on your device only. Game statistics and ads, described below, work the same for guests.

b. If you create an account

  • Email address and password, handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). Passwords are stored by Google in hashed form; I can't see them. Firebase also processes your IP address and device information to keep sign-in secure.
  • Account ID: a random identifier Firebase creates for your account.
  • Game data, stored in Google Cloud Firestore: your progress, Affection, Bestiary, Coins, Gems, items, win streaks and purchase-granted items.
  • Social data: your username, Friend Code, friends list, gifts sent and received, and leaderboard scores with their dates and times. Your username and leaderboard scores are visible to other players. Please don't use your real name or personal details as a username.

c. Advertising

Fruity Fauna only shows an ad when you choose to watch one for a reward. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay, Unity's ad mediation service, which picks an ad from ad networks such as Unity Ads and Google AdMob. These services may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. They use this to show ads, limit repeats, measure performance and prevent fraud. Third parties may place and read cookies, web beacons or similar identifiers for this purpose. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Unity privacy policy.

The game also includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK, which tells Meta when the game is installed and opened, along with device information and your advertising identifier. This lets me see which of my ads on Facebook and Instagram bring in new players. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

On iOS, you're asked for permission before your advertising identifier is used for tracking, and you can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

d. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. I receive confirmation that a purchase happened (product, date and a transaction ID) so the game can deliver it. I never receive your name, card number or billing address from them.

e. Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how Fruity Fauna is played, so I can fix problems, balance difficulty and improve the game. It records what happens in the game, for example:

  • starting, winning and losing boards, and feeding animals
  • earning and spending Coins and Gems, and using items
  • opening the Bestiary and seeing offers
  • choosing to watch a reward ad, and how much the ad earned
  • purchases (the product and its price, never your payment details)
  • your progress at the time, such as your level and how many animals you've fed

With these events, Google Analytics automatically collects a random ID created when the game is installed (the app-instance ID), your device's advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking), your device model, operating system, language and app version, how long and how often you play, and your approximate location (country, region and city), which is worked out from your IP address. Google Analytics doesn't store your IP address.

Game statistics never include your email, password, username or account ID, and they aren't linked to your account. Google signals and data sharing with other Google products are turned off, so Google processes this data only to provide these statistics to me. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

f. If you contact me

If you email support, I receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.

2. How information is used

  • To run the game, save and restore your progress and deliver purchases.
  • To provide friends, gifts and leaderboards.
  • To show ads that help keep the game free.
  • To fix crashes, prevent cheating and fraud, and improve the game.
  • To answer your support requests.

Where the law requires a legal basis: account and game data are used to provide the service you asked for. Game statistics rely on my legitimate interest in understanding how the game is played so I can fix and improve it. Ads rely on my legitimate interest in keeping the game free and, on iOS, on your tracking permission where it applies.

4. How long information is kept

  • Account and game data: until you delete your account. It is then removed within 30 days, and from Google's backups within 180 days.
  • Guest data: on your device until you uninstall the game.
  • Support emails: up to 1 year after your issue is resolved.
  • Game statistics (Google Analytics): detailed event data is deleted automatically after 14 months. Summary reports, such as the number of players per day, don't identify anyone and may be kept longer.
  • Ad data: as described in each ad provider's policy.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time: here's how.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask me to correct it, by emailing service.wyliolivete@gmail.com.
  • Limit ad tracking in your device settings, as described above.
  • Game statistics: deleting your account in the game also resets the analytics ID on that device, so new statistics can't be connected to earlier ones. Because game statistics aren't linked to your name, email or account, I can't look up one player's past statistics. They are deleted automatically after 14 months.

Depending on where you live (for example under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, the EU/UK GDPR or California law), you may have further rights, such as to object to or restrict processing. I'll respond within 30 days. If you're in the Philippines, you can also contact the National Privacy Commission.

6. Children

Fruity Fauna is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email me and I'll delete it.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google's security measures. No method is 100% secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the game or on this page.
